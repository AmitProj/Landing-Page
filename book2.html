Maya has spent ten years avoiding the one place she promised never to return to. But when a box of forgotten letters arrives at her doorstep, she is forced to confront the words she left behind.

✦ Behind the pages

Hello, I'm Elena.

I have always been fascinated by the little moments people overlook — a goodbye at a train station, an old photograph in a drawer, a message typed but never sent.

Those moments became my stories.

I started writing in a tiny apartment with one notebook, one stubborn idea and far too much coffee. Years later, I'm still doing exactly the same thing.

When I'm not writing, you'll find me wandering through bookstores, collecting old postcards, drinking tea and pretending my next chapter is already written.
