The Last Letter

✦ Fiction ✦ 342 Pages ✦ First Edition

Twenty years after leaving her hometown, Clara receives a letter that should never have existed — written by the one person she promised herself she would forget.

What begins as a search for answers becomes a journey through old photographs, unfinished promises and a love story that refused to disappear.

About The Author

Hello, I'm Elena.

I'm a novelist, storyteller and lifelong collector of sentences written in the margins of notebooks.

My stories are inspired by ordinary people, quiet places, complicated relationships and those tiny moments that somehow change everything.

When I'm not writing, you'll probably find me reading an old novel, walking somewhere beautiful or drinking far too much coffee.
