Questions

Insurance questions, explained simply.

How do I know which insurance I need? +

It depends on your circumstances, assets, responsibilities, risks and local requirements. Start by identifying what you want to protect and then discuss suitable coverage options.

Is the cheapest policy always the best? +

Not necessarily. Premium is only one part of a policy. Coverage limits, deductibles, exclusions, conditions and service should also be considered.

Can I combine different insurance policies? +

Depending on the insurer and market, different policies may be available through the same provider or advisor. Bundling can sometimes be an option, but the details vary.

What information should I prepare? +

Prepare basic personal or business information, details about the property or vehicle where relevant, existing policy information and your preferred coverage goals.

Can I ask questions before buying? +

Absolutely. Asking questions about coverage, exclusions, limits, deductibles, premiums and claims is an important part of making an informed insurance decision.
